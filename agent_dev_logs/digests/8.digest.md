## 🎯 Goal
Dana asked, after reviewing the ALTO results, for each category's share of the page next to its count, so pages of
different sizes compare. Five columns, her names kept:

| Column                                                  | Definition                             |
|---------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| `clear_rat`, `noisy_rat`, `trash_ratio`, `non-text_rat` | the category / (`num_lines` − `Empty`) |
| `empty_rat`                                             | `Empty` / `num_lines`                  |

Then the statistics are regenerated for the whole corpus from the classified line CSVs that exist, checked, and the
run reported (scope, outputs, counts, errors, skips). Nothing else changes.

## 🧭 Where it stands — 2026-10-22

**Done on `test`:**
- `aggregate_STAT.py`: one column list, `PAGE_STATS_COLUMNS`, for both paths of `_sum_metrics` (with and without a
  scoreable line; it was written out twice), and `_add_category_ratios()`, called by both. The five columns sit
  after `Empty`, rounded to 4 places. A zero denominator gives an empty cell (`NaN`): an `Empty`-only page has
  `empty_rat` 1.0 and the other four empty; `num_lines` 0 leaves all five empty.
- `tests/test_aggregation.py`: 8 tests (a mixed page, no `Empty`, `Empty` only, `num_lines` 0 through a category
  outside `STANDARD_COLS`, the no-text path, range and rounding, both paths' columns, the old columns unchanged).
- The six `data_samples/DOC_LINE_STATS*/` files regenerated.
- Docs: `docs/reference/outputs.md` Step 4.2 (formulas, the undefined cases, read by name), `docs/usage.md`,
  `data_samples/README.md`.
- The rerun: `tools/page_stats_rerun.sh` (SLURM, on the pattern of `alto_util/cpu_job.sh`) and
  `tools/page_stats_check.py` with 7 tests.

**Three findings:**
1. **The averages are not rounded.** `docs/usage.md` said "all numeric averages are rounded to 4 decimal places",
   which an older aggregator in atrium-alto-postprocess did; the code since this repository began (10-02), and so the
   10-12 corpus run, writes them as computed (`0.9457999999999999`). The issue asks the ratios to round "consistently
   with the existing statistics": the ratios round to 4, the averages stay as they are (rounding them would change
   existing statistics), and the doc now says so.
2. **The shipped sample stats were stale against the shipped line CSVs.** Regenerating them changed more than the
   new columns: in `DOC_LINE_STATS/`, `avg_quality_score` on 4 of the 7 pages and the `Clear`/`Noisy` counts of
   `CTX000000001` p.1 (its lines were re-categorised after the stats were written); `DOC_LINE_STATS_gpt/` came from
   the older aggregator (averages rounded, an `avg_symbol` column). The cause is the committed files, not this change:
   the code before and after #8 gives the same old columns on the committed line CSVs. The two
   `*_page_stats_SHORT.csv` excerpts are from an older run too and stay as they are, noted in `data_samples/README.md`.
3. **The column positions move.** The ratios come after `Empty`, so `total_word_count` and everything after it shift
   by five. Consumers read by name (the 1210 hand-over note says so); a positional reader must be updated.
