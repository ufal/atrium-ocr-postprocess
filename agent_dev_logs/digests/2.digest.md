## 🎯 Goal
Calibrate the line categories (`Clear`, `Noisy`, `Trash`, `Non-text`, `Empty`) against human review of real output, so that lines keep `Trash` away from enrichment and the page average is a usable quality signal.

## 🧩 What exists (`v1.12.0-beta`)
- **Gates and tiers:** a pre-filter, then a language tier, then the quality score and the gate order, all described step by step in `docs/categorization_logic.md`, whose worked-example table is generated from the walkthrough tests.
- **Calibration tooling:**
  - `tools/corpus_change.py` (`recat`, `diff`, `score`, `pack`, `sample`, `reach`, `pagecheck`);
  - `tools/recategorize_from_csv.py --gold-column gold_categ`;
  - `tools/ab_constant_eval.py` (exact McNemar);
  - `tools/gold/` (sidecars, `GOLD.md`).
- **Final output:** `DOC_LINE_CATEG_1210` for the ARÚP and ARÚB collections (stage 18 of #3, checks passed).
