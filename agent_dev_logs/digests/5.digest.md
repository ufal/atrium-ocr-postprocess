**Two findings recorded with it, for the reader to know before signing off:**
1. `CATEG_NOISY_SCORE_MAX` (0.80) does not decide `Clear` vs `Noisy`; it only clamps the stored score. The split is the valid-word ratio (< 0.85) and damaged tokens.
2. **The Czech-first heuristics** act on every line, whatever its language label: the function-word list, `w`/`q`/`x` weirdness, the rotation penalty, the all-caps code rule and the Latin-only vowel class.
   - So clean English, German and French lines are treated unevenly: `The excavation was carried out in 1932.` reads `Noisy`.
   - They are pinned as `known_limit` cases and not changed, being frozen for the pilot.
   - The document ends that section with the post-pilot options and how to measure each with `tools/corpus_change.py`.

**Earlier answers recorded in the document** (from #3, then alto#30, `v1.8.0-beta`): `Trash` means illegible (Q1); web and e-mail addresses are `Clear` (Q4); a word on the `[allowed]` list is never evidence of damage (Q5a/Q5b); `Trash` stays for the shape-witness route (Q6).
